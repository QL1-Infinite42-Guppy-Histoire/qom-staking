QOM Staking

Stake QOM with QL1 validators and earn yield. Signing is done with Keplr or FoxWallet.

Stake and earn yield

When you stake QOM with a validator, you earn rewards (yield) from the network.

Your stake helps secure the network, and you receive rewards in return. The reward rate is not fixed and varies with network conditions.

Fees are shown on the wallet's confirmation screen for each operation. Reward amounts vary with chain conditions (they are not fixed). Reads go through a public QL1 node. Private keys are handled only inside your wallet and are never passed to this site. You can also stake with MetaMask (it signs Cosmos-format transactions using a signature method called EIP-712).
